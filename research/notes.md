## 6. The Friction List (The Sales Pitch)

1. **Massive Journey Fragmentation**:
   A follower seeing a drool-worthy post on Instagram has to bounce through Linktree, select from 4 different delivery links or an in-store pickup link that takes them to Square, only to find that Square is for 24h preorders, with another link redirecting them back to Order.online for same-day orders.
2. **Pre-order vs. Same-Day Ambiguity**:
   The primary pickup link leads to Square Online which warns customers about 24h lead times, confusing same-day customers who just want a box in 20 minutes.
3. **Location-First vs. Menu-First Barrier**:
   Linktree forces customers to choose their town before they even know what flavors are on the menu this month.
4. **App Download Tax**:
   Bio links heavily emphasize downloading a native iOS app just to see doughnuts, creating unnecessary friction for spontaneous web traffic.
5. **Abandoned / Outdated Pages**:
   The existing Square site's `/locations` page displays placeholder San Francisco addresses and placeholder phone numbers, eroding customer trust.
6. **The "Comment 'menu'" Disconnect**:
   When Instagram reels say "Comment MENU and we'll DM you the link", sending users to a multi-link Linktree creates drop-off at every tap.

**The Solution in This Demo**:
One unified, responsive web experience on their own domain. Instant menu exploration, seamless pickup ordering across Auburn, Westborough, Worcester, and Millbury with a fast box builder, an executive multi-location dashboard, and single-click access for Instagram bio and comment automation.

## 7. Design Direction (Monochrome Redesign, November 2026)

Rebuilt from a reference screenshot (a bold food brand site), keeping its layout language but with a **black-and-white interface** in place of its orange and cream. Food photos stay in colour.

- **Type**: Archivo (variable, via `next/font`). Display = width 62, weight 800–850, tight tracking (`.cond`, `.cond-xl`); body = width 100. The dashboard uses a calmer width-100 cut for headings so numbers stay legible.
- **Palette** (`tailwind.config.ts`, `app/globals.css`): ink `#161616`, paper `#ececea`, chalk `#f7f7f6`, mist `#d9d9d6`, graphite `#3b3b39`, smoke `#878784`. Every hue palette Tailwind ships (red, emerald, amber…) is remapped to neutral, so the interface itself can't pick up colour. Photos and doughnut drawings keep their natural colour, with no filters. Legacy `rocco-*` tokens still exist and point at the new greys; `rocco-red` follows `--accent` (ink on light, white on `.on-ink` / `.dash` surfaces).
- **Layout**: ink nav band → inset rounded photo frame for the hero → pale product cards in a horizontal rail with a progress scrubber → big rounded photo panel → alternating ink / mist bakery cards → mist form panel with underlined fields → ink footer with a giant wordmark. Inner pages open with a shared `PageHeader` (giant word + one sentence + one fact).
- **Buttons**: `Pill` component (`src/components/ui/Pill.tsx`): a circle of the inverse tone rises on hover while the label rolls.
- **Motion** (GSAP + ScrollTrigger + SplitText, all inside `gsap.matchMedia` with a reduced-motion branch):
  - Preloader (`src/components/Preloader.tsx`), home page only, once per session: "ROCCO'S" cut out of an ink cover fills bottom-up with the hero photo while a counter runs 000→100, then the mask dives into the stem of the R and the photo becomes the hero.
  - Hero chars rise through masks; on scroll the photo pushes in and the title lifts away.
  - Statement paragraph inks in word by word with inline photo pills; photo panel opens from a clipped window; footer wordmark rises; rotating stamp turns with scroll; CSS photo marquee.
- **Photos**: all Unsplash stand-ins, listed in `src/data/photos.ts`. Swap in the bakery's own shots there.
